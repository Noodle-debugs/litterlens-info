LitterLens Downloader

A personal tool that saves the owner's SiiPet pet-camera videos to the owner's Google Drive, organized by date and cat.

The downloader runs on GitHub Actions. Google authorization is limited to files created by or explicitly used with this app. It skips videos already saved and updates filenames when a manual cat assignment changes.

Videos are retained for up to 90 days or until the active collection reaches 10 GB. Older videos are moved to Drive's bin and permanently removed after 7 days.

This is a personal project, not an official SiiPet or Google product. It is not offered as a public service.
